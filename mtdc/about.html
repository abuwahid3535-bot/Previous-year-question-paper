About the Portal

Mother Theresa Degree College's student portal gives students and staff a single place to access examination question papers, educational resources, exam results, and college contact information.

  • Browse and download previous question papers by course, semester, year or subject.
  • Check your published exam results and grading summaries.
  • Staff members can upload question papers and study documents for student use.
  • Update your profile and change your password securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in?
Go to the Login page. Students use their registered email and password (the Student option). Staff and administrators use the Staff option with their college email and password.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the Login page. Enter your registered mobile number and follow the OTP verification steps to set a new password.
Where do I find question papers?
Open the Home page and use the search section to filter by course, semester, year or subject, then click View or Download. You must be logged in.
How do I see my exam results?
After logging in as a student, the "My Results" section on your dashboard shows the marks published for you, including an overall score summary.
A staff account says it is pending. What now?
New staff accounts are reviewed by the administrator. Wait for approval; you will be able to log in once the account is approved.
How can I contact the college?
Use the Contact page to send a message through the portal, or visit the office hours listed there.
